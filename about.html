Our Story

Built on the ground, one consignment at a time.

Founded in 1996, Rajat Carrying Corporation was established with a simple promise—to deliver every shipment safely, on time, and with complete accountability.

Over the past 30+ years, we've grown into a trusted logistics partner for manufacturers and industrial businesses, providing reliable Full Truck Load (FTL) and Part Truck Load (PTL) transportation across India.

Based in GIDC Vapi, we combine our own fleet with a dependable transport network to ensure flexibility, timely deliveries, and seamless logistics solutions for businesses of every size.

At Rajat Carrying Corporation, we don't just move goods—we build long-term partnerships through transparency, reliability, and a commitment to keeping our customers' operations moving without interruption.

"Every shipment reflects our commitment to reliability, precision, and the trust our clients have placed in us for over three decades."
